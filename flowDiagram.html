IRCTC Improved Booking Flow

flowchart TD
    Client[Booking client] --> Edge[JWT at edge]
    Edge --> Ingress[Regulated token ingress]
    Ingress --> App[Stateless application servers]
    App --> TTL[Sliding TTL Window]
    TTL --> Redis[Redis + Lua atomic TTL extension]
    Redis --> Review[Reviewing]
    Review -->|Seat available| OnHold[On-Hold: temporary seat lock]
    Review -->|No seat available| Expired[Expired]
    OnHold -->|Proceed to payment| Paying[Paying]
    OnHold -->|holdTimer elapsed| Expired
    Paying -->|holdActive| Confirmed[Confirmed]
    Paying -->|hold inactive| Expired
    Confirmed --> Terminal((Terminal))
    Expired --> Terminal
